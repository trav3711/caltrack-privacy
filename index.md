# CalTrack Privacy Policy

*Effective 11/22/2026*

CalTrack is a calorie and weight tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it. If you have questions, contact trav.cwelch@gmail.com.

## The short version

- Your food logs, weight logs and profile live on your iPhone.
- When you ask for a calorie estimate or a weekly summary, the food details needed for it are sent to an AI service to produce the answer. CalTrack's server doesn't keep them.
- An account is optional. If you sign in with Apple, your food and weight logs are backed up to CalTrack's server so they sync across your devices.
- There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and your data is never sold.
- You can delete your account and everything backed up to it from Settings at any time.

## Information stored on your iPhone

CalTrack stores the following on your device:

- **Your profile:** sex, age, height, weight, activity level, weight goal and daily calorie goal. These are used to calculate your calorie target.
- **Food logs:** what you ate, calories, meal, the calorie breakdown, time logged, and any photo you attached.
- **Weight logs.**
- **Favorites** and **weekly summaries** you've generated.
- **Settings**, such as your units and reminder preferences.

This information stays on your iPhone unless you use the features described below. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Calorie estimates and weekly summaries

When you tap **Estimate calories**, CalTrack sends the food description you typed and, if you added one, the photo to CalTrack's server. The server forwards them to **Anthropic**, the AI company that makes Claude, which returns the estimate.

When a **weekly summary** is created, CalTrack sends that week's food log to the same service: food names, calories, meals, breakdowns and dates, along with your daily calorie goal and weight goal.

CalTrack's server passes this information through to produce the result and does not store it. Anthropic processes it to generate the response under its commercial terms and privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Your name, email and Apple account are not included in these requests.

## Optional account (Sign in with Apple)

You can use CalTrack without an account. If you choose **Sign in with Apple** in Settings, CalTrack's server stores:

- **Your Apple user identifier** and **email address**. If you chose "Hide My Email", this is a private relay address from Apple.
- When the account was created and last used.
- **Session tokens**, which keep you signed in. They are stored in hashed form.
- **Your food and weight logs**, so they can sync between your devices and carry over to a new phone. Food photos are **not** uploaded; they stay on the device where you took them.

This data is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts CalTrack's server and database.

## Reminders and notifications

Reminders are optional and are scheduled on your iPhone. CalTrack does not use a server to send them.

## Technical information

To protect the service from abuse, CalTrack limits how many calorie estimates and weekly summaries can be requested per day. For this, the server keeps a daily count for your device's IP address and for a random identifier the app sends (Apple's "identifier for vendor", which isn't linked to your name or Apple account and changes if you delete and reinstall the app). These counts are deleted after about two days and aren't linked to your logs or your account. The server also uses your IP address to limit requests per minute. Like most web services, Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs for security and reliability.

## Who we share information with

- **Anthropic**, only for calorie estimates and weekly summaries, as described above.
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts CalTrack's server and stores account data.
- **Apple**, when you use Sign in with Apple, TestFlight or the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

## Deleting your data

- **Account and backed-up logs:** in CalTrack, go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This deletes your account, every session, and all food and weight logs stored on CalTrack's server, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Logs on your iPhone are kept until you delete them or the app.
- **Everything on your iPhone:** delete the CalTrack app.
- You can also email trav.cwelch@gmail.com to ask for your data to be deleted.

## Health information

CalTrack's calorie numbers are estimates, some generated by AI, and its summaries are general information. CalTrack is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet.

## Children

CalTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.
